    Data notes

    What counts as a deal?

    Each deal groups all outcomes held in one Polymarket market. A market closes when each outcome has fewer than 0.01 shares remaining. The close time is the last recorded position event, shown in your local time zone.

    How is profit calculated?

    We add Polymarket’s reported realized P&L across the market’s outcomes. Open-market marks, deposits, withdrawals and the estimated value of dust are excluded. The account total includes any manual activity in this trading account.

    How fresh is the data?

    A read-only publisher on the bot host checks every 15 minutes. Trade changes publish on the next successful check. Quiet periods receive a timestamp update every six hours. The footer shows the snapshot time.
